What it’s for

A place to run the month, not a spreadsheet graveyard.

Most money apps either hide the bills behind transactions or lecture you about lattes. Cove is the list you already keep — cars, meds, cards — with math that updates when you check something off.

01

See the repeating load

Living, subscriptions, and debt mins in separate bands. Not mixed in with last Tuesday’s coffee.

02

Paid changes the leftover

Mark a $25 min paid and unpaid drops $25. Cashflow moves. Extra on a debt does not fake the bill total.

03

Debts belong next to bills

Minimums, extras, payoff math. Change a min on Debts and Monthly expenses follows.

04

A simple P&L

What came in, what the month costs, what you still owe. No accounting homework.

How it feels

Three numbers. Then the list.

Income on the left. Unpaid in the middle. Planned net and live cashflow on the right — split so you can see the plan vs what you’ve actually sent out.

  • Skip a sub this month and cashflow stays higher
  • Throw extra at a card without inflating monthly expenses
  • Start alone. Add a second login later if you ever want a shared view
How setup works

“I don’t need another bank login. I need to know if this month still works.”

That’s the product.

Setup

Three moves. Then it’s your month.

Step 1

Add income

Paycheck, recurring or not. Commission stays off the month until you add it.

Step 2

Dump the repeating load

Cars, meds, streaming, card minimums. Living, subs, and debts stay in their own bands.

Step 3

Check things off

Paid drops unpaid and live cashflow. Extra is extra — it only hits cashflow.

Ready

Stop rebuilding the month in Excel.

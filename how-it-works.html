How it works

Start alone. Invite later.

Individual

1

Open the portal. No password in this prototype.

2

Add accounts and a few recurring bills.

3

Log spending. Categories suggest themselves. Budgets fill in.

Couple

1

Add a partner. They get their own login.

2

Shared dashboard becomes home. Personal stays a tab.

3

Mark accounts visible or hidden. Split expenses. Settle when you want.

This prototype opens on a sample couple — Alex and Jordan — so every screen already has data. Switch users in the portal header to see privacy in action.

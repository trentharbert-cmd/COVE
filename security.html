Security

No bank passwords. Ever.

This first version is manual on purpose. Nothing connects to a real bank.

What v1 stores

Demo transactions you type in the browser. Sample household data. No live account credentials.

If we add bank sync later

You authenticate at your bank through a licensed aggregator (like Plaid). Cove would receive tokens and transaction data — never your bank password.

Partner privacy

Personal accounts are visible by default. You can hide an account from your partner. Hidden accounts never appear on their shared or personal views.

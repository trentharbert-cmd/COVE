Features

Everything in the first version.

Built so you can test the product, not imagine it.

Shared + personal

Household pulse and your own view. Hide an account from your partner when you want.

Shared expenses

Add household items even if your partner never logs in. Split, assign to one person, or mark Joint.

Monthly expenses

The repeating load: rent, utilities, meds, memberships — due dates and paid status.

Transactions

The ledger across accounts. This month, last month, year to date, plus spend analysis.

Debts

Balances, minimums, extra payments, months remaining, and payoff tips.

Financials

P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, budgets, and goals in one place.

Settle-up

Who owes whom, from the shared dashboard — not a separate chore list.

Income that flexes

Add a paycheck or commission. Mark it recurring only if it actually repeats.

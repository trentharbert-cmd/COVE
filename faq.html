FAQ

Quick answers.

Do I need an account to try this?

Not in this prototype. Enter portal goes straight in. Use the user switcher to see Alex, Jordan, or an individual view.

Can my partner see everything?

Shared items and visible personal accounts, yes. Hide any personal account so it stays off their screen.

Does it connect to my bank?

Not in v1. Everything is manual. Bank linking would only happen later through a third-party aggregator — never by typing bank passwords into Cove.

How do splits work?

Default 50/50. Change the percent or assign who pays. Settle-up keeps a running balance.

Is Cove a real company yet?

This is a working product draft so you can click through the idea and change it.

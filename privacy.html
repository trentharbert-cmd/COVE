Privacy

What we store, and what we don’t

Last updated September 1, 2026. This is a working product, not a bank.

Account

Email and a hashed password live in Supabase Auth. We do not see your password. Reset and invite mail is sent by Supabase.

Household data

Bills, debts, income, transactions, and month snapshots you type are saved to your household so you can open them on another device. A partner you invite can see shared household data. Personal debts stay on your seat unless you share them.

What we don’t have

No bank passwords. No Plaid connection yet. No sale of your ledger to advertisers.

Your choices

You can clear household data in Settings. You can request account deletion by emailing the address you used to sign up — reply to a reset mail for now until support exists.
